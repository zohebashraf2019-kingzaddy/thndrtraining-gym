THNDR TRAINING WEBSITE

Open index.html to preview, or upload the entire folder to GitHub/Vercel/Netlify. Keep assets folder alongside index.html.

Included: responsive multi-section website, trainer profiles, photo grid with next/previous arrows, membership cart (localStorage), discount information, Google map, English/Spanish story, contact inquiry form.

IMPORTANT BEFORE LAUNCH:
1. The inquiry form opens a mail draft with NO recipient because no official gym email was supplied. Connect Formspree, EmailJS or a backend, or replace with a verified recipient. It does not currently send inquiries.
2. The cart is a DEMO only; it does not charge, reserve memberships, or securely store customer details. Connect a real payment provider and backend after approval.
3. Trainer biographies are placeholders. Add verified biographies later.
4. Confirm schedule, prices, discount stacking, payment methods, and permission to use uploaded photos with the gym.
5. All images were converted to optimized WebP, preserving their framing and uniformly displayed via CSS object-fit:cover.
